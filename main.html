Summary

Dhurandhar is a two-part Hindi espionage action-drama directed and written by Aditya Dhar, released across parts in December 2005 (Dhurandhar) and March 2026 (Dhurandhar: The Revenge). The film draws on real-world Indian counter-terrorism history, blending historical events like the 1999 IC 814 Kandahar hijacking, the 2001 Indian Parliament attack, and the 26/11 Mumbai attacks into a high-stakes intelligence narrative.
